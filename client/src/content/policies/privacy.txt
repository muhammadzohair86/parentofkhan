# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 4, 2026**

At Parent Of Khan, we respect your privacy. When you place an order or use our website, you share some personal information with us. We only use that information to run our store, process your orders and stay in touch with you when needed.

### What information do we collect?

When you place an order, we may ask for your name, phone number, email address and delivery address. If you create an account, we also store the information needed to manage it.

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we'll keep your email address so we can send you store updates and offers.

### How do we use your information?

We use your information to:

- Confirm and process your orders.
- Arrange delivery and share the necessary details with our courier.
- Contact you about your order or any issue related to it.
- Manage your account and respond to your questions.
- Send store updates and offers if you've signed up for them.

We don't sell your personal information to other businesses.

### Orders and payments

Our current checkout process uses WhatsApp. Once you submit your order on our website, we save the order details and open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. You can review that message and decide whether to send it to us.

Opening WhatsApp does not automatically send the message. We only receive it if you choose to send it.

We don't ask you to enter your card number, CVV or banking password through this checkout process. Our team will share the available payment instructions with you when discussing your order.

If a payment provider or bank is involved in a transaction, its own privacy policy and security practices will also apply.

### Who do we share information with?

We only share the information needed to provide our services. For example, our courier needs your name, phone number and delivery address to deliver your parcel.

If you contact us through WhatsApp, your messages are also handled by WhatsApp, which is operated by Meta. Their own privacy policy applies to that communication.

### Keeping your information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect the information stored on our website. Access to customer information is limited to people who need it to manage orders and provide support.

No website or online service can promise complete security, but we do our best to keep your information protected.

### Your choices

You can contact us if you want to update or correct your personal information. You can also ask us about the information we hold about you.

If you no longer want to receive promotional emails, you can unsubscribe. You may also request deletion of your personal information, although we may need to keep certain order or transaction records for legal, accounting or security reasons.

### Children's privacy

Our website is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

### Changes to this policy

We may update this page when our website, services or the way we handle information changes. Any updates will be posted here with a revised date.
