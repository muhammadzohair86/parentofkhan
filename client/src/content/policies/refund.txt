# Refund & Return Policy

We want you to be happy with your purchase from Parent Of Khan. But if something isn't right with your order, here's how you can request a return or refund.

### 1. Return period

You can contact us within **7 days of receiving your order** if you'd like to request a return or refund. Requests made after 7 days may not be accepted.

### 2. Product condition

To be eligible for a return, the item must:

- Be unused and unworn.
- Be in its original product packaging.
- Have all original tags attached.
- Be returned with the invoice or proof of purchase.

You can open the courier's outer bag or box to check your order. However, the product itself should remain unused and in its original packaging.

### 3. Items that cannot be returned

For hygiene and product-condition reasons, we may not accept returns of items that have been worn, washed, damaged after delivery or returned without their original tags or packaging.

If an item arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, please contact us so we can look into it. These cases will be reviewed separately.

### 4. Return shipping charges

For a change of mind or a return that isn't due to a mistake on our part, the customer will be responsible for the return courier charges.

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect item, please contact us before sending it back. We'll discuss the next steps with you.

### 5. How to request a return or refund

Simply contact us through WhatsApp or our customer support contact details and share your order reference along with the reason for your request.

Our team will review your request and guide you through the return process. Please don't send an item back until we've confirmed the return instructions.

### 6. Refund method

Once your returned item reaches us and passes inspection, we'll contact you to arrange the refund.

Refunds will be sent through an agreed payment method, such as a bank transfer, Easypaisa or JazzCash. We may ask for the relevant account details to complete the transfer.

### 7. Refund processing time

After we receive and inspect the returned item, please allow up to **15 working days** for the refund to be processed.

We'll let you know if there is any issue that may delay the process.

### 8. Need help?

If you have any questions about a return or refund, get in touch with us and we'll do our best to help.
