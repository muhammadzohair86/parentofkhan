Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Thanks for shopping at Parent Of Khan. We deliver orders within Pakistan and to selected international destinations. Here's what you need to know about how we handle shipping and delivery.

1. Order processing

Once you place an order through our website, our team will review the details and contact you through WhatsApp to confirm it.

Orders are prepared for dispatch after confirmation and completion of the required payment arrangements. Processing and dispatch may take additional time during weekends, public holidays or busy periods.

We'll share any important updates about your order through the contact details you provide.

2. Shipping charges and delivery estimates

Shipping charges are calculated at checkout based on your delivery destination and the weight of your order. The applicable charges will be shown before you complete your order.

Within Pakistan

We currently arrange domestic deliveries through NLS Xpress.

Delivery charges and estimated delivery times depend on the destination and parcel weight. The shipping cost shown at checkout is the amount applicable to your order.

International orders

International shipping charges depend on the destination and parcel weight. The applicable cost and estimated delivery time will be shown during checkout.

International orders may also be subject to customs duties, import taxes or other charges imposed by the destination country. These charges are the customer's responsibility and are not included in our shipping fees unless clearly stated otherwise.

3. Delivery address

Please double-check your name, phone number, complete address, city and country before submitting your order.

For international orders, make sure your phone number includes the correct country code.

If you need to correct your address, contact us as soon as possible through WhatsApp. Once a parcel has been handed over to the courier, we may not be able to change its delivery details.

4. Tracking your order

Once your parcel has been dispatched, you can contact our team for its tracking details, where available.

Delivery and tracking updates depend on the information provided by the courier.

5. Delays and unsuccessful deliveries

We do our best to get every order delivered on time, but sometimes delays happen. Weather conditions, public holidays, busy shipping periods, customs checks and courier-related issues can affect delivery times.

If a parcel cannot be delivered because the customer is unavailable, the address is incorrect or the parcel is not collected, it may be returned to us.

Any additional shipping charges for resending such a parcel may be the customer's responsibility.

6. Damaged, missing or lost parcels

If your parcel arrives damaged, or you believe it has been lost during delivery, please contact us as soon as possible.

Share your order reference and, if the parcel is damaged, clear photos of the outer packaging and the item. We'll review the issue and work with the courier where necessary.

7. Contact us

If you have questions about shipping, delivery charges or the status of your order, feel free to contact us.
